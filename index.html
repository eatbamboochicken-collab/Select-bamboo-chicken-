WRITTEN METHODS
TECHNIQUE

FRYING METHOD

Pan-frying technique for a crisp, golden bottom finish

  1. 1
    Add oil to a hot pan.
  2. 2
    Place chilled dumplings flat-side down into the pan and fry until the bottoms turn crisp and golden brown.
  3. 3
    Add a few reasonable drops of water if needed, keeping the focus on achieving a crisp bottom.
  4. 4
    Continue frying until the dumplings are thoroughly heated through with a crisp, golden finish. Serve hot.
TECHNIQUE

STEAMING METHOD

Gentle cooking technique for tender wrappers and juicy filling

  1. 1
    Line a steamer basket with baking parchment paper or clean cabbage leaves.
  2. 2
    Arrange dumplings spaced slightly apart so they do not touch as they cook.
  3. 3
    Place steamer over boiling water, cover with lid, and cook until piping hot throughout. Serve immediately.
